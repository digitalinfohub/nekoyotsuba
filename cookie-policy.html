Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Digital Infohub ("we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on digitalinfohub.net (the "Site"). It works alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember preferences, and to provide information to site owners. Similar technologies include pixels, scripts and local storage.

2. How We Use Cookies

At present, this Site relies only on strictly necessary functionality needed to display pages and does not set tracking or advertising cookies itself. However, third-party services embedded or linked from the Site may set cookies in your browser:

  • Google Fonts: requests to load web fonts pass basic technical data (such as IP address) to Google's servers.
  • Pollinations.AI: our free AI tool communicates with an external API; standard web headers apply.
  • External platforms: if you click through to GameDistribution, Game Jolt, itch.io, Fab, ArtStation or other partner sites, their own cookie policies apply from that point onward.

3. Advertising & Analytics Cookies (Future Use)

If we enable advertising (e.g. Google AdSense) or analytics tools in the future, additional cookies may be used by us and by third-party vendors, including Google, to serve ads and measure performance based on your visits to this and other websites. When that happens:

  • We will update this policy and, where required by law, ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.
  • You will be able to opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info/choices.

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

Blocking cookies may affect how some websites function, but the core content of this Site remains accessible.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as the Site evolves. The revised date at the top of this page indicates when it was last changed.
