Frequently Asked Questions

      

        

          Is this really free?
          
Yes. The tool uses the free Pollinations.AI service and there is nothing to install, no sign-up and no payment.

        

        

          Why do I sometimes see "temporarily unavailable"?
          
The free public AI service occasionally gets busy or rate-limited. When that happens we show the message "Free AI via Pollinations is temporarily unavailable, please come back later". Just try again in a minute or two.

        

        

          Can I use the generated code in my own projects?
          
The generated games are single HTML files you can download, open and edit in any text editor. They're great for learning how simple browser games work. As with all AI output, review the code before publishing it anywhere.

        

        

          Does the tool store my prompts?
          
Nothing is stored on this website — your prompt is sent directly from your browser to the AI service when you press Generate. See our Privacy Policy for details.

        

        

          What makes a good game description?
          
Mention the genre (platformer, puzzle, shooter…), the controls (keys/mouse/touch), the objective (score, survive, collect) and a visual style (pixel, neon, pastel, minimal). Short classic concepts work best — you can always regenerate with tweaks.
