Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Infohub ("we", "us", "our"), operating the website at digitalinfohub.net, handles information when you visit and use our website and its free tools. We respect your privacy and aim to keep our practices simple and transparent.

1. Information We Collect

Digital Infohub is designed to be usable without creating an account. We collect only limited information:

  • Information you give us voluntarily: If you email us (for example to services@digitalinfohub.net or artifyspace@icloud.com), we receive your email address and the content of your message so we can respond.
  • Technical information: Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic server data such as IP address, browser type, referring pages and access times. This data is used for security and basic site operation, and is not used to personally identify visitors.
  • AI tool prompts: When you use our Free AI Game HTML Coder, the text you type is sent directly from your browser to a third-party AI service (Pollinations.AI) to generate your result. We do not store your prompts on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries, service requests and messages you send us.
  • To operate, maintain and secure the website and its tools.
  • To understand general usage (e.g. which pages are popular) so we can improve content.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

3. Cookies

This website currently uses only the cookies that are strictly necessary for it to function. If we later introduce analytics or advertising technologies (such as Google AdSense), they may use cookies. In that case:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices.

Please also see our Cookie Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Some features involve communication with trusted third parties from your browser:

  • Pollinations.AI — powers the Free AI Game HTML Coder. Your prompt text is transmitted to their service to generate code. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — web fonts may be loaded from Google's font CDN, which receives your IP address as part of any standard web request.
  • External links — our site links to partner platforms such as GameDistribution, Game Jolt, itch.io, Fab and ArtStation. Once you click through, those sites' own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for third-party websites.

5. Advertising (Google AdSense)

If and when advertising is displayed on this website, it may be served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising principles and the explanation of how Google uses data are available at policies.google.com. This Privacy Policy will be updated whenever advertising features are enabled.

6. Data Retention

Emails and enquiries are retained only as long as needed to answer them and to maintain business records. Server logs are managed by our hosting provider according to their standard retention settings. We do not sell any personal information, ever.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is general-audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of any website. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR rights if you are in the EEA/UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example when new features are added. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
